# CLAUDE.md — Targeted Value-First Content Engine

## 🛠 Channel Mission & Flywheel
- **Core Strategy:** Solve viewer pain points using a specific tool, product, or framework, then transition them into an engaged ecosystem (feedback loops, community, or paid education).
- **The Targeted Value Engine:**
  1. **Audience Mapping:** Identify the exact persona experiencing the problem.
  2. **The Hook:** Trigger that persona's acute, specific pain point.
  3. **The Flagship Solution:** Introduce the product/tool/course feature that solves it.
  4. **The Practical Proof:** Step-by-step implementation showing the tool in action.
  5. **The Funnel Loop:** Convert attention into feedback, discussion sessions, or enrollments.

---

## 📜 Audience & Scripting Principles
- **No Generic Content:** Every video must be engineered for a *specific* sub-segment of viewers. If a tool helps three different audiences, make three separate videos targeting their unique frustrations.
- **Speak Their Language:** Use the exact terminology, industry jargon, or internal frustrations that the target audience uses when complaining about their problem.
- **Feature-to-Outcome Mapping:** Never list product features in isolation. Every flagship function must be directly linked to eliminating a specific pain point identified in the audience map.
- **Anti-Patterns to Avoid:**
  - *Never* use a broad "Hey everyone" intro. Call out the specific target audience or their exact situation in the first 5 seconds.
  - *Avoid* over-complicating the demo. Keep the practical instructions tailored to the target audience's skill level (e.g., beginner-friendly vs. advanced-pro).

---

## 💻 AI Command Frameworks

When prompting the AI, enforce this exact 2-step sequence:
1. `[Map Audience]`: Before scripting, profile the target viewer. Output: *Persona Name, Core Frustration, Current Inefficient Workaround, and Emotional Trigger.*
2. `[Generate Targeted Script]`: Use the mapped audience profile to write a 3-act script (Targeted Pain -> Flagship Function Demo -> Specific Funnel CTA).

## 🎨 Creative & Funnel Conventions

### 1. The Targeted Video Blueprint
- **Phase 1: Persona-Targeted Hook (0-15%):** Call out the situation and agitate the pain. (e.g., *"If you are an indie author spending 3 hours a day manually checking Amazon BSR..."*)
- **Phase 2: Flagship Pivot (15-30%):** Introduce the tool/course feature as the specific antidote to that pain.
- **Phase 3: Practical Implementation (30-85%):** A contextual, real-world walkthrough solving the exact problem mentioned in the hook.
- **Phase 4: Targeted Funnel CTA (85-100%):** 
  - *Feedback Loop:* "Struggling with a different edge case in your workflow? Comment below."
  - *Upsell:* "To get the full automated template and join our deep-dive implementation session for [Persona Type], link is in the description."

### 2. Funnel Description Architecture
- **Line 1-2 (Above the fold):** [Persona-Specific Benefit Link: e.g., "Automate your BSR tracking here: URL"]
- **Line 3-4:** [The Feedback/Community Call: URL]
- **Remaining Copy:** Structured timestamps, persona-relevant keywords, and resources.
